## 1. VidRango — Video Processing SaaS

**Website:** [vidrango.com](https://vidrango.com/)  
**Focus:** SaaS architecture · media processing · workflow automation · product engineering

**The problem:** Publishing video across multiple platforms takes repeated editing, formatting and export work.

**The system:** A single workflow for preparing video outputs for different publishing formats, with processing steps including transcription, captions, voiceover, smart reframing and final output preparation.

**Engineering areas:** Next.js, Node.js, PostgreSQL, FFmpeg, background jobs, APIs, media orchestration and operational monitoring.

**What this demonstrates:** Turning a complex multi-step workflow into a product with clear user steps, processing states, error handling and reusable automation.

## 2. Kinivalo — Custom eCommerce & Operations

**Website:** [kinivalo.com](https://kinivalo.com/)  
**Focus:** Custom PHP · MySQL · payments · delivery APIs · fraud/risk controls

**The problem:** An online storefront needs reliable checkout, payment status, shipping workflows and order-management processes.

**The system:** A custom eCommerce experience with bKash payment integration, Pathao delivery workflows, internal order/admin tools and checkout risk controls.

**Engineering areas:** PHP, MySQL, REST APIs, webhooks, payment callbacks, order-state handling, NGINX and production operations.

**What this demonstrates:** Integrating customer-facing commerce with the back-office systems that make daily operations work.

## 3. PurePetsFood — WordPress & WooCommerce

**Website:** [purepetsfood.com](https://www.purepetsfood.com/)  
**Focus:** WooCommerce · product content · catalog workflows · on-page SEO

**The problem:** Product catalogs need consistent content, images, structure and discoverability.

**The system:** A WooCommerce-based store with product-management workflows and search-focused product presentation.

**Engineering areas:** WordPress, WooCommerce, product data, responsive layouts, technical/on-page SEO and content workflows.

**What this demonstrates:** Combining eCommerce implementation with product operations and search visibility.

## My approach

1. **Understand the goal:** Users, business constraints and the desired outcome.
2. **Design the system:** Data models, integrations, states, security and operational needs.
3. **Build and test:** Functional workflows, edge cases and responsive behavior.
4. **Deploy and monitor:** Hosting, reliability, performance and recovery paths.
5. **Improve:** Usability, automation, SEO and conversion-related bottlenecks.
